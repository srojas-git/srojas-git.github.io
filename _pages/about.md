I'm a **Technical Product Owner** at [Konrad Group](https://konrad.com), where I bridge business strategy, engineering, and data to turn ambiguous requirements into shippable product outcomes. I bring a Scrum-expert foundation together with hands-on technical fluency — REST APIs and JSON, relational and non-relational databases, cloud environments, and the DEV/UAT/PROD release lifecycle — so I can speak both languages: business intent and engineering constraint.

I care deeply about Developer Experience (DX), owning the end-to-end path from requirement to release, and I stay close to how GenAI and backend architecture are reshaping what's buildable. Before Konrad, I spent close to three years as a Business Presentations Specialist at **McKinsey & Company**. I'm currently pursuing a degree in Data Science Engineering at Universidad Fidélitas.

This site showcases the intersection of product ownership, data, and engineering: case studies, requirements frameworks, and tools that reflect how I take a product from ambiguity to delivery.
